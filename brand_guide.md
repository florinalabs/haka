# HAKA — Brand Identity Guide

## Tagline
**Isipin. Tuklasin. Umangat.**
*Ideas That Move People & Business.*

## Mission
A Filipino digital media and innovation brand connecting youth, entrepreneurs, professionals, and tech enthusiasts with cutting-edge content on **AI, Technology, Business, and Modern Lifestyle**.

## Target Audiences

| Audience | Focus Areas | Content Style |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| **YOUTH** (Gen Z, Students) | Career skills, future careers, digital lifestyle | Trendy, relatable, engaging |

| **TECHNOLOGY** (Professionals, enthusiasts) | Gadgets, emerging trends, tools | Exploratory, future-focused |
| **AI** (All levels) | Tools, automation, practical applications | Demystifying, hands-on |

## Brand Voice: **Taglish** 🇵🇭

### Tone & Style
- ✅ **Modern & Relatable** – Speak like your Gen Z peers
- ✅ **Simple Explanations** – Break down complex topics
- ✅ **Practical Value** – Actionable insights, not just theory
- ✅ **Curiosity-Driven** – "What's changing? What comes next?"
- ✅ **Authentically Filipino** – Taglish natural flow

### Voice Guidelines
```
DO:
"Ano ang mga AI tools na pwede mong gamitin para mag-double up ng productivity?"
"Hindi lang tech—kailangan mo ring alam kung paano ito ginagamit sa totoong mundo."

DON'T:
"Ang Artificial Intelligence ay isang transformative technology na..."
(Too formal, too academic)
```
