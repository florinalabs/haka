## What Needs to Be Created

### 1. Logo Design
**Requirements:**
- Modern, clean design
- Works in both dark and light backgrounds
- Scalable (vector format preferred)
- Must be recognizable as a Filipino tech brand

**Concept Ideas to Explore:**
- Text-based: "HAKA" with stylized lettering
- Icon + text combination
- Abstract representation of "thinking/discovering/ascending" theme
- Consider incorporating Filipino cultural elements subtly

**Color Preferences (Pending):**
- Primary colors TBD
- Should feel modern and tech-forward
- Accessible contrast ratios

---

### 2. Brand Colors
**Color Palette Needed:**

#### Primary Colors
- Main brand color (for logos, headers)
- Secondary accent color
- Gradient options for digital use

#### Neutral Colors
- Backgrounds (light & dark)
- Text colors
- Borders and dividers

#### Status/Action Colors
- Success (green)
- Warning (yellow/orange)
- Error (red)
- Information (blue)

---

### 3. Typography

#### Primary Font
- Modern, readable for digital screens
- Works well in both Tagalog and English contexts
- Has good Latin + Philippine script support

Options to consider:
- Sans-serif (clean, modern): Inter, Poppins, SF Pro Display
- Or a custom blend of Latin + Filipino fonts

#### Hierarchy
- Display / Heading sizes
- Body text
- Caption size
- Code/technical font monospace alternative
