# 📱 HAKA — Social Media Guide

## Multi-Platform Strategy for Filipino Digital Media

---

## Platform Overview

### YouTube (Main Hub) 🎬
**Role:** Primary content destination, long-form educational content  
**Frequency:** 2 videos/week  
**Format:** 
- Long-form: 5-10 minutes (tutorials, deep dives)
- Shorts: 30-60 seconds (quick tips, hooks)

**Best Practices:**
- Strong thumbnails with text overlays
- First 30 seconds must hook immediately
- End screens promoting related content
- Community posts for engagement between uploads

**Upload Schedule:**
- Monday: Technology/Tools focus
- Wednesday: Business/SME content  
- Friday: Youth/Future trends or community Q&A

---

### TikTok (Viral Reach) 🎵
**Role:** Discovery, short-form viral content  
**Frequency:** 3 posts/day  
**Format:** 15-60 second reels

**Best Practices:**
- Hook in first 2 seconds (text overlay + visual)
- Trending audio when relevant
- Use trending effects sparingly but strategically
- Hashtags: 3-5 max, mix broad + niche

**Posting Times (Philippines):**
- Peak 1: 7-9 AM (commute time)
- Peak 2: 12-1 PM (lunch break)
- Peak 3: 6-8 PM (evening scroll)

---

### Instagram (Community + Visuals) 📸
**Role:** Visual storytelling, behind-scenes  
**Frequency:** 
- Feed posts: 3-4/week
- Stories: Daily
- Reels: 2-3/week

**Best Practices:**
- High-quality visuals (carousel for tutorials)
- Stories: polls, Q&A boxes, countdowns
- Reels: same strategy as TikTok
- Highlights: "Tutorials", "About HAKA", "Testimonials"

---

### LinkedIn (Professional Audience) 💼
**Role:** Business/enterprise focus, B2B content  
**Frequency:** 2-3 posts/week  

**Best Practices:**
- Professional but accessible tone
- Industry insights and case studies
- Tag Filipino business leaders when relevant
- Engage with comments meaningfully

---

### Facebook (Community Building) 👥
**Role:** Filipino community, older demographic  
**Frequency:** 4-5 posts/week  

**Best Practices:**
- Mix of video + text content
- Join and post in relevant groups
- Host live Q&A sessions monthly
- Share user-generated content

---

## Cross-Platform Content Strategy

### Content Repurposing Workflow

```
1. CREATE (YouTube Long-form)
   └── Deep dive video: 5-10 min
   
2. EXTRACT (Content from long-form)
   ├── Hook clip → TikTok/Reels
   ├── Key quote → Instagram graphic
   ├── Summary thread → LinkedIn carousel
   ├── Discussion question → Facebook post
   
3. ENGAGE (All platforms)
   ├── Reply to all comments within 2 hours
   ├── Pin important comment/question
   ├── Share in relevant groups/pages
```

## Engagement Guidelines

### Response Time Targets
| Platform | Target Response Time | Priority |
|----------|---------------------|----------|
| YouTube Comments | Within 4 hours | High |
| TikTok Comments | Within 1 hour | High |
| Instagram DMs | Within 2 hours | Medium-High |
| Facebook Comments | Within 6 hours | Medium |
| LinkedIn Posts | Within 30 minutes (if viral) | High |

### Engagement Best Practices

#### DO:
- ✅ Reply to comments with questions
- ✅ Pin valuable comment threads
- ✅ Share viewer success stories
- ✅ Cross-promote relevant content
- ✅ Use polls and Q&A stickers in Stories

#### DON'T:
- ❌ Delete negative feedback without context
- ❌ Auto-reply to all comments (feels robotic)
- ❌ Ignore questions asked by viewers
- ❌ Over-use hashtags (looks spammy)
- ❌ Post same content identically everywhere

## Crisis Management

### When Things Go Wrong

#### Scenario: Negative Comments on AI Claims
**Response:**
1. Acknowledge the concern respectfully
2. "You raise a great point. We're always learning more about this topic..."
3. Provide sources or invite discussion in comments
4. Don't delete genuine concerns (builds trust)

#### Scenario: Technical Issues with Video
**Response:**
- Post update on Stories/Community tab
- "Technical issues! Working to resolve. New upload coming soon!"
- Apologize briefly, focus on solution
